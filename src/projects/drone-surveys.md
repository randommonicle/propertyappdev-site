## What the drone is for

Routine condition checks on the parts of a building that are hard to see from the ground, such as roof coverings, gutters and the upper parts of a facade. A ground-level survey misses a lot of that, and scaffolding costs far too much for a routine look. The high-resolution imagery goes straight into maintenance planning and contractor briefings.

## The reporting side

After a flight I have photographs and my own recorded commentary. Both go to Claude, which drafts a structured report to a defined template. That has cut the time between finishing a survey and having a usable written report, and it keeps the reports consistent from one building to the next. The same approach, spoken notes and photos turned into a finished report, is what the [property inspection app](/projects/property-inspection-app/) does for walk-round inspections.
